About — Who We Are

SiteSale GeoFix What Is It?

SiteSale GeoFix is a free online tools website built for website owners, bloggers and especially people who want to sell or buy a website. A website's real value depends on its SEO, the quality of its traffic and the visitors coming from the target country — and checking those three things is often difficult and expensive.

To make this problem easy, we have brought 12 free tools together in one place. No tool asks you for money, and you don't need to create an account: just enter the values and get the result in seconds. Every result also comes with simple advice in plain English, so you understand not just the number, but what it means.

For Website Sellers

Before selling a site, it is important to know its value, the quality of its traffic and the state of its SEO. The Website Value Calculator and Traffic Quality Check prepare you for the buyer's questions in advance, so the deal closes at a good price.

For US Traffic

Traffic from the US is the most valuable, because AdSense CPC and buyers' purchasing power are highest there. The Geo Traffic Analyzer shows what percentage of your traffic comes from the US, UK or Pakistan, and also gives advice on how to grow it.

For Better SEO

To rank on Google, using the title, meta description and keyword correctly is the foundation. The US SEO Checker and Keyword Density Checker score your pages and explain the reason behind every weakness, so fixing them is easy.
